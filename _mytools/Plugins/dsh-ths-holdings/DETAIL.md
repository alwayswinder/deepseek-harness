# Portfolio P&L

A floating card in the DSH web GUI: it syncs your real positions from the Tonghuashun investment ledger and shows today's P&L, the Shanghai Composite, and the intraday chart, refreshed every 20 seconds.

This page manages the data behind the card — where the holdings file sits, its export date, the total assets, and how the holdings table is sorted.

Installation, how the card behaves, where the data comes from, where the settings live, and troubleshooting are in `README.md` in the plugin directory.
